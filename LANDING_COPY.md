## FAQ

**Q: Is Cost IQ free?**
A: Yes — the audit runs 100% in your browser with no account required. There's no free tier with limits — it's genuinely free.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No. You can run an audit immediately. We only ask for an email after you've seen your results, and only if you want to save a shareable link and receive a copy by email.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: Prices are sourced directly from vendor pricing pages and verified on 2026-05-22. All source URLs are listed in PRICING_DATA.md. Estimates are conservative — they compare your current plan against cheaper alternatives from the same vendor or competitors. Actual savings may be higher if you negotiate directly.

**Q: What does Cost IQ do with my data?**
A: Nothing until you choose to share your results. The audit engine runs entirely in your browser — your spend data never leaves your device. If you submit your email, we save your audit results to generate a shareable link and send you a confirmation email. We never sell data or spam.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex is a marketplace for discounted AI credits. For high-spend teams (>$500/month on AI tools), buying credits through Credex instead of direct retail can cut costs by 15–25%. Cost IQ surfaces a Credex option when your savings opportunity is large enough to warrant it.

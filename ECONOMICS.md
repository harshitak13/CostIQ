# Unit Economics — Cost IQ

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

A "converted lead" for Credex means a startup founder or engineering manager who completes a Cost IQ audit, provides their contact information, and eventually books a consultation or purchases Credex credits for AI procurement optimization.

**Estimated lead value: $150–$500 per qualified lead.**

Reasoning: The average startup team of 10 engineers spends $2,000–$8,000/month on AI tooling (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, API costs). A 20–30% optimization on this spend translates to $400–$2,400/month in savings. If Credex captures 10–15% of the first year's savings as its fee (standard for procurement optimization), that's $480–$3,456 in revenue per customer. Assuming a 30% close rate from qualified lead to paying customer, each lead is worth roughly **$150–$500** depending on team size.

For enterprise leads (50+ engineers), this number jumps to $2,000–$10,000 per lead, but Cost IQ's initial target is seed-to-Series-B startups where the sweet spot is teams of 5–25 engineers.

## Conversion funnel

```
Visitors land on Cost IQ                     1,000 (100%)
  ↓
Complete the spend audit form                  350 (35% — low friction, no signup)
  ↓
View full audit results                        300 (86% of starters — instant results)
  ↓
Provide email for detailed PDF report          120 (40% of viewers — lead captured)
  ↓
Book a Credex consultation call                 24 (20% of leads — high intent)
  ↓
Purchase Credex credits / become customer        7 (30% of consultations — closed deal)
```

**Key conversion levers:**
- **Audit completion rate (35%):** Keep the form to ≤3 steps. No signup required. Show instant value.
- **Lead capture rate (40%):** Gate the AI-generated summary and PDF export behind email. The free audit shows enough value to make the email feel worth it.
- **Consultation booking (20%):** Triggered by showing "You could save $X/year — talk to a Credex expert to implement these changes." High-savings audits (>$500/month) convert at 30%+.

## What makes this $1M ARR in 18 months

**Target: $83,333/month in recurring revenue by month 18.**

**Model assumptions:**
- Average customer value: $300/month (mid-market startup, 10–20 engineers, annual contract)
- Churn rate: 5%/month (typical for SMB SaaS; offset by expansion revenue as teams grow)
- Net revenue retention: 110% (teams add engineers → spend grows → Credex captures more)

**Growth math:**

| Month | Monthly audits | Leads captured (40%) | Customers added (5.8% of leads) | Cumulative customers | MRR |
|-------|---------------|---------------------|--------------------------------|---------------------|-----|
| 1–3 | 500/mo | 200 | 12 | 34 | $10,200 |
| 4–6 | 2,000/mo | 800 | 46 | 160 | $48,000 |
| 7–12 | 5,000/mo | 2,000 | 116 | 550 | $165,000 |
| 13–18 | 10,000/mo | 4,000 | 232 | 940 | $282,000 |

**Cumulative by month 18:** ~940 active customers × $300/mo = **$282K MRR ($3.4M ARR run rate).**

This exceeds the $1M ARR target. Even at 50% of these projections (conservative), that's $141K MRR = **$1.7M ARR** — still above target.

**What makes this achievable:**
1. **Zero-friction top of funnel.** No signup, instant audit. Removes all barriers to the first "aha moment."
2. **Built-in virality.** Every audit produces a shareable URL. If 10% of users share their results and each share brings 2 new users, the viral coefficient is 0.2 — meaningful organic growth.
3. **SEO moat.** "Cursor vs Copilot pricing," "AI tool cost for startups," "how much should I spend on AI" — these queries are growing 300%+ YoY with very little quality content targeting them. Cost IQ becomes the authoritative answer.
4. **Enterprise expansion.** A single IC runs the audit → shares with their EM → EM runs it for the whole team → CTO sees the report → Credex gets an enterprise lead. The product sells bottom-up.
5. **Credex's existing distribution.** If Credex has an existing customer base or partner network, embedding Cost IQ as a free assessment tool in their sales flow dramatically reduces CAC and accelerates pipeline.

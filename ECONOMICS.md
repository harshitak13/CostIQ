# Unit Economics — Cost IQ

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

A "converted lead" = someone who books a Credex consultation
and buys AI credits.

Reasoning:
- Credex sells discounted AI credits at, say, 20–30% below retail
- A startup spending $500/mo on AI tools might buy 6 months
  of credits upfront = $3,000 order
- Credex margin on resale: assume 15–20% gross margin
- Revenue per converted customer: $3,000 × 17.5% = ~$525 gross profit
- With repeat purchases (quarterly): LTV over 12 months ≈ $525 × 4 = ~$2,100

Estimated lead value (converted): $500–$2,100 depending on spend size.
Blended conservative estimate: $800 per converted customer.

## Conversion funnel

Step 1: Landing page visit → audit started
  Target: 40% start rate (tool is free, low friction)

Step 2: Audit started → audit completed
  Target: 70% completion (form has localStorage,
  no account required)

Step 3: Audit completed → email captured
  Target: 35% capture rate
  (value shown first, email asked second)

Step 4: Email captured → consultation booked (high-value only)
  Target: 15% of high-value leads (>$500/mo savings)
  book a Credex call

Step 5: Consultation booked → credit purchase
  Target: 40% close rate on consultations

Combined funnel (visit → purchase):
  40% × 70% × 35% × 15% × 40% = 0.59%
  i.e. roughly 1 in 170 visitors becomes a paying Credex customer

## What makes this $1M ARR in 18 months

ARR target: $1,000,000
Avg revenue per customer per year: $800 (gross profit, see above)
Customers needed: 1,000,000 / 800 = 1,250 customers

Working backwards from the funnel:
- 1,250 customers needed over 18 months = ~70/month by month 18
- At 0.59% visit-to-customer rate:
  70 / 0.0059 = ~11,900 visits/month needed at peak
- At 40% audit start rate: ~4,750 audits started/month
- At 35% email capture: ~1,660 leads/month
- At 15% high-value rate: ~250 high-value leads/month
- At 40% close: ~100 new customers/month

Month 1–3: 0–50 customers (launch + HN + IH)
Month 4–9: 50–400 customers (SEO kicking in, X virality)
Month 10–18: 400–1,250 customers (referrals + outbound)

What has to be true:
1. Shareable result URLs drive meaningful organic traffic
   (virality coefficient > 0.3)
2. Credex consultation close rate holds at ≥ 40%
3. Average customer spend is ≥ $500/mo in AI tools
   (i.e. we attract the right users, not hobbyists)
4. SEO content around "AI tool cost comparison"
   drives 3,000+ monthly visits by month 9
